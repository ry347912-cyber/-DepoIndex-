Topic Segmentation & Digression Isolation

Handling unconstrained deposition dialogue requires resolving three distinct boundary challenges:

  • Procedural Digression Isolation: Regex pattern anchors cleanly isolate lunch recesses, sidebars, and objections without topic bleeding.
  • Multi-Page Continuations: Hierarchical clustering aggregates contiguous testimony spanning up to 8 pages into unified topic nodes.
  • Non-Contiguous Re-entry: Tracks when testimony returns to an earlier subject (e.g. Contract Negotiations returning post-lunch).
