🖼️ DepoIndex Technical Architecture & Executive Deck
1. Architecture & System Overview

DepoIndex is an AI-powered legal deposition indexing system designed to convert unformatted transcript text into a verifiable, chronologically ordered topic index with exact Page and Line addressability.

  • Transcript Parsing Engine: Indexes raw text line-by-line into a strict coordinate map (Page X, Line Y) ↔ Text.
  • Hierarchical Topic Segmenter: Detects topic boundaries, multi-page continuations, procedural digressions, and non-contiguous re-entries.
  • Deterministic Provenance Verifier: Audits AI candidate entries against verbatim transcript coordinates to eliminate hallucinated page/line references.
2. Topic Segmentation Approach

Depositions lack explicit topic headings and contain complex conversational flows. DepoIndex resolves boundary challenges through a 3-tier approach:

  • 1. Procedural Digression Isolation: Uses regex pattern anchors (e.g. "THE VIDEOGRAPHER: We are off the record") to cleanly separate lunch breaks, sidebars, and objections.
  • 2. Multi-Page Continuation Tracking: Employs two-pass hierarchical clustering to aggregate contiguous pages discussing the same core subject matter.
  • 3. Non-Contiguous Re-entry Linking: Identifies when an attorney returns to an earlier subject (e.g. Contract Negotiations returning post-recess on Page 33).
